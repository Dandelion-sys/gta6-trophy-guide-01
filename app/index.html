Road to the platinum

About 200 hours, one playthrough, no online trophies. PowerPyx rates the set 4 out of 10. Cheats disable trophies and saving, so keep them off.

  1. 1

    Finish the story — 60–80 hours

    Five chapters. Play however you like; progress toward the 100% checklist already counts. Keep a manual save at the start and end of each chapter.

    0 of 12 story trophies

  2. 2

    Side quests & activities — 100 hours

    Questlines for Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone and Bret, the strangers, the races, stunts and ranges. Most of it feeds the 100% checklist.

    0 of 8 side-quest trophies

  3. 3

    Cleanup to 100% — 10–40 hours

    Open Progress > Stats > 100% Completion after the story. It is closer to 60% true completion, with only 70 of 80 challenges needed. Nothing on it can be missed.

    0 of 2 cleanup trophies

⚠ One trophy can be missed: Positive Mental Attitude. In Chapter 1, after the mission Down to Earth, talk to Lucia in the kitchen in the morning, follow her to the balcony and accept her meditation lesson. That home meditation is gone after Chapter 1.
